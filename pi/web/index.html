Your own audio

Upload audiobooks, ripped CDs or the kids' own recordings straight to the box — no subscription, works offline. Group files into a collection, then add it below as a library entry using the folder path shown.

Follow a Spotify profile

The section mirrors that profile's public playlists: make a playlist public in the Spotify app and it shows up on the box by itself; make it private and it disappears. Private playlists, Liked Songs and Spotify-made editorial lists are never visible. Needs the one-time Spotify API setup in install.sh.

Storytel audiobooks

Add your Storytel account under Settings first.

One tile per series. Books download to the box and play offline — the phone only fetches them. Add a book to your shelf in the Storytel app, then check it here to send it to the box.

Settings

On: Wi-Fi skips its power-save naps during Bluetooth playback — fewer radio handovers that can glitch the Bluetooth chip, at the cost of roughly 15–20% shorter listening time on battery. Irrelevant when Wi-Fi is off entirely.

Changing quality restarts Spotify on the box — if music is playing it stops and resumes from the same spot on the next play. Best quality streams about twice the data of normal; already cached songs are unaffected.

Spotify

–

Generated on Spotify's developer dashboard by the account this box should play for (Premium). Saved on the box only; the engine restarts with it.

The box locks to one account so no passing phone can take it over. Switch account opens it briefly: pick the box under Devices in the Spotify app (same Wi-Fi) with the account you want — it logs in, stays remembered, and re-locks on its own.

Storytel

–

Stored only on the box, to download the audiobooks you pick. Positions can sync back as a backup; the box never reads them back, so a book heard on your phone won't move the box. Google/Apple sign-in accounts don't work here — use e-mail and password.

Bluetooth speaker

–

Put the speaker in pairing mode first. “Scan” lists nearby devices so you can pick one; “Pair nearest” automatically connects to the single audio device in pairing mode (~30–60 s). “Pair from car” makes the box visible for ~2 minutes — start the pairing from the car’s Bluetooth menu, then pick the car as speaker when it appears above.

Wi-Fi

–

“Save network” stores it without needing it in range — pre-add the cabin or grandparent Wi-Fi before travelling, and the box joins it by itself on arrival. Joining another network moves the box: reconnect your phone to that network and reopen this page (vibb.local or the new IP). “Reconnect” turns the radio back on and restarts the auto-off timer — same as toggling Wi-Fi on from the box’s screen.

This phone

Settings, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and shutdown need this phone to be linked to the box. On the box: Settings → Link phone, then scan the QR code. Playback works either way.

Backup

Keep a copy of the library, settings, logins and every child’s place in every book — so a dead SD card costs an evening, not the whole box.
